Your practice loop

How it works

The goal is progress—not perfection. Review your transcript and personalized feedback, then use it to improve your next attempt.

1

Choose

Browse a role-play scenario and select a customer conversation to practice.

2

Practice

Speak naturally, listen actively, and build rapport like you would with a live customer.

3

Review

Use the transcript and AI feedback to identify strengths, opportunities, and what to change.

4

Repeat

Apply the feedback, test a new opening, value statement, or objection-handling technique.
